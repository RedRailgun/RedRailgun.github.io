# The Yuchengco Centre — Pre-Launch Leasing Website

**Client:** The Yuchengco Centre (TYC)\
**Site in progress:** [tyc.cmdev.cloud](https://tyc.cmdev.cloud)

## Overview

The Yuchengco Centre is a soon-to-open skyscraper along Sen. Gil Puyat Avenue in Makati, and a sister project to our earlier work on the A.T. Yuchengco Centre. The brief was to build a leasing website for a building that doesn't exist yet — no finished lobby to photograph, no tenants to quote, no ribbon-cutting to point to. The site's job is to sell prospective BPO and corporate tenants on a workspace they can only see in renders, while carrying the same design language established for the client's earlier tower.

## The Challenge

Leasing a building before it opens means the website has to do the persuading that a finished, occupied space would normally do on its own. Renders can look thin or generic if the site around them doesn't earn its own credibility. The task was twofold: extend a design language that already existed for a related property so the two feel like one family, and give a pre-launch building the visual weight of a high-quality brochure rather than a placeholder page waiting for real photography.

## Design Approach

- **Carry over the established design language.** Dark backgrounds with gold accents echo the client's earlier A.T. Yuchengco Centre site and mirror this new tower's own gold facade — so the two properties read as one family under the same corporate hand.
- **Serif headings against a geometric sans body.** Serif type gives the "visionary workspace" messaging a more editorial, considered feel, while body copy stays clean and easy to scan for specs and contact details.
- **A sidebar in place of a nav bar.** Home, Amenities, Retail, Property, Location, Leasing, and About sit in a fixed vertical rail — a quieter, more architectural way to navigate that leaves more of the page free for full-bleed imagery.
- **Full-bleed, brochure-style sections.** Large image blocks for the lobby, executive lounge, business center, and park carry the pacing of a printed brochure — one idea, one striking visual, per scroll.
- **Let the renders do the work the building can't yet.** With no finished interiors to photograph, high-quality architectural renders of the lobby, executive bar, and park spaces stand in for real photography, styled consistently enough to feel authoritative rather than speculative.
- **Sustainability as a credibility anchor.** A dedicated section on green infrastructure and the Philippine Green Building Code, plus targeted LEED, WELL, WiredScore, and SmartScore certifications, gives prospective tenants a concrete reason to believe in the building beyond the visuals.
- **Leasing inquiry kept close at hand.** Contact details and an inquiry form sit persistently in the header and near the page end, so a tenant convinced by the renders can act immediately.

## Outcome

The result extends the visual identity built for the A.T. Yuchengco Centre into a second property, so the Yuchengco name reads as one consistent brand across both buildings rather than two disconnected sites. For a tower that hasn't opened yet, the site borrows the pacing and polish of a printed brochure to make renders feel like a finished, confident preview rather than a placeholder — giving the leasing team a credible tool to bring in tenants well ahead of the building's doors opening.
