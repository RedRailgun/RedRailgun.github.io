# Cardinal Santos Medical Center — Website Redesign

**Client:** Cardinal Santos Medical Center (CSMC)\
**Live site:** [cardinalsantos.com.ph](https://cardinalsantos.com.ph)

## Overview

Cardinal Santos Medical Center is a long-established Metro Manila hospital whose old website had grown, page by page, into an unmaintainable WordPress site — years of ad hoc additions with no consistent structure underneath. The redesign wasn't a cosmetic refresh; it was a full re-architecture of the site's information, paired with a visual system that finally matched the "YOU are family" warmth CSMC wanted patients to feel.

## The Challenge

The core problem was structural before it was visual. Years of unplanned page creation had left the site's navigation messy, with related content scattered instead of grouped — patients and doctors alike couldn't reliably find what they needed. On top of that, the brand's signature red carried an unfortunate double meaning in a healthcare context: it reads as blood and danger before it reads as brand. The task was to **rebuild the site's structure with clear, related groupings, while finding a way to use the client's own red so it felt reassuring rather than alarming.**

## Design Approach

- **Re-architected navigation around clear sub-groups.** Pages were audited and reorganized into logical categories — Services, Patients' and Visitors' Guide, Academic Programs, Careers, About Us — so related content lives together instead of being buried across disconnected pages left over from years of piecemeal updates.
- **Softened red with warm gradients.** Rather than fields of flat red or clinical white, subtle warm gradients sit behind sections throughout the site, taking the edge off red's danger association and giving pages a lived-in, human warmth instead of a sterile, soulless feel.
- **Large page banners with faded photography.** Every page opens with a full-width banner using a desaturated, faded photo as a backdrop — introducing color and humanity through imagery rather than more red, and giving each section its own visual identity beyond the brand palette.
- **Retained brand red and typeface as functional anchors.** The existing red and font stayed exactly where they belong — navigation, CTAs, section headers, icons — so the site is unmistakably CSMC, while photography and gradient carry the emotional warmth red alone couldn't.
- **Consistent modular sections for repeatable content.** Patient stories, services, packages, and news all follow the same card and carousel patterns site-wide, so the structure that was rebuilt on the back end reads as consistent, predictable rhythm on the front end too.
- **Surfaced key online transactions as homepage CTAs.** Three link cards — Find a Doctor, E-Consult, and Get Results — sit just below the hero banner, giving CSMC's modernized online services top billing. Getting results online in particular lets patients and doctors skip the wait in line entirely, and putting it front and center on the homepage makes sure that convenience doesn't get lost in the navigation.

## Outcome

The rebuilt site replaced a tangled, hard-to-maintain WordPress structure with clearly grouped, findable pages — patients can now locate services, guides, and doctor information without hunting across leftover pages from years of ad hoc updates. Visually, the hospital's signature red survived the redesign intact but recontextualized: framed by warm gradients and photography instead of standing alone, it now reads as brand consistency rather than a clinical warning. The result feels organized, warm, and unmistakably "family" — while remaining a hospital site patients and staff can actually navigate.
