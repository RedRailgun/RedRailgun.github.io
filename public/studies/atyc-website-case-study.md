# A.T. Yuchengco Centre — Corporate & Leasing Website

**Client:** A.T. Yuchengco Centre (ATYC)\
**Live site:** [atyc.com.ph](https://atyc.com.ph)

## Overview

A.T. Yuchengco Centre is a 34-storey Grade A office building in the heart of Bonifacio Global City. The client needed a corporate website that could host its own leasing inquiries directly — giving prospective BPO and corporate tenants a clear path to property details, building features, and contact, without relying on a broker's listing page. The brief called for a site that felt **professional, bold, modern, and elegant** — matching the stature of the building itself.

## The Challenge

A Grade A office tower is, at its core, a commercial real estate product — the risk with most corporate leasing sites is that they read as generic brochureware: stock photography, safe layouts, nothing that ties back to the building itself. The challenge was to give the site a visual identity as distinctive as the building's architecture, while keeping the actual job of the site — communicating specs, amenities, and how to lease — fast and easy to find.

The building itself gave the answer. At night, a sharp vertical line runs up the tower's facade and lights up — the architectural signature of ATYC. The design task became: **translate that single architectural detail into a digital identity**, rather than inventing a generic brand language from scratch.

## Design Approach

- **A vertical line as the site's signature motif.** The same sharp vertical accent from the building's night lighting is carried through as a recurring strip across the site — a quiet, consistent thread that ties every page back to the physical tower.
- **Bold display type for headings, geometric sans for body copy.** Headlines use a bold, confident typeface to carry the "exquisite, modern" tone the client wanted, while body text sits in a clean geometric sans — keeping specs, addresses, and contact details easy to scan.
- **Full-bleed architectural photography.** The building is the hero. Large, high-contrast shots of the tower's glass facade do the work of conveying scale and quality, rather than leaning on illustration or stock imagery.
- **A tight, function-first navigation.** Property, Amenities, Tenants, Leasing, Contact — five destinations, no clutter, so a prospective tenant can get from landing page to a leasing inquiry in as few clicks as possible.
- **Leasing contact surfaced everywhere, not buried.** Phone and email for leasing inquiries appear in the top utility bar and the footer on every page, so the site's core commercial purpose is never more than a glance away.

## Outcome

The result is a site that feels architecturally specific to ATYC rather than a template with the logo swapped in — the vertical line motif and bold/geometric type pairing give it a distinct, elegant identity, while the simplified navigation and persistent leasing contact keep the commercial goal front and center. Prospective tenants can move from first impression to a leasing inquiry without friction, and the building's own design language now lives on in its digital presence.
