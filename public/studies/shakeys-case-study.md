# Shakey's Pizza Asia Ventures — Corporate Website Redesign

**Client:** Shakey's Pizza Asia Ventures, Inc. (PIZZA)\
**Live site:** [shakeysgroup.ph](https://www.shakeysgroup.ph)

## Overview

PIZZA is a publicly-listed holding company whose corporate site has to serve two audiences at once — investors reading governance and financial disclosures, and the general public who know the company through its restaurant brands (Shakey's, Peri-Peri, Potato Corner, R&B Tea, Project Pie). The redesign also coincided with Shakey's 50th anniversary in the Philippines, adding weight to the brief. True to Shakey's own "Fun. Family. Pizza." tagline, the goal was for the site to feel like it belongs to a **family-friendly restaurant group**, not just a listed company filing reports — while still giving shareholders the clarity they need.

## The Challenge

The company's Annual & Sustainability Report is bold, colorful, and illustrated — full of flat character art and color-blocked sections. A literal translation of that onto a compliance-heavy IR site risked burying financial tables and governance content under decoration. The real task wasn't "add color," it was **deciding where personality earns its place and where it needs to get out of the way.**

## Design Approach

- **Contain the vibrant brand color to navigation and footer.** The red header and dark footer carry Shakey's identity on every page, so the brand is never absent — but it frames the content rather than competing with it.
- **Shift main content to white and pastel backgrounds.** Dense pages (governance, disclosures, shareholder rights) sit on white with muted cream and yellow used only for section dividers and tab states, keeping long-form text and tables legible.
- **Use color functionally, not decoratively.** Numbered circles for shareholder rights, pill-shaped tabs for active sub-navigation, and color-coded accordion borders per stakeholder group all use the palette to guide the eye — the same job color does in the annual report's chaptering, just quieter.
- **Push illustration to the edges.** Playful graphics (pizza slices, fries, boba) appear as corner accents on select sections, echoing the report's character art without overwhelming regulatory content.
- **Reserve full brand energy for brand-facing pages.** The homepage keeps the vibrant, photo-led, illustrated treatment the client wanted people to feel first — while investor and governance sub-pages stay restrained and scannable.

## Outcome

The result is a deliberate compromise between the report's book-style richness and a web interface built for scanning and finding things fast: one consistent brand frame (nav/footer), a calmer content layer for compliance-heavy pages, and full-color storytelling reserved for the pages meant to build brand warmth. Governance and disclosure pages stayed fully intact and easy to navigate, while the homepage and brand pages carry the "family-friendly" energy the client asked for.
