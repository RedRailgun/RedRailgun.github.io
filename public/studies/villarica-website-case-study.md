# Villarica Pawnshop — Corporate Website Redesign

**Client:** Villarica Pawnshop, Inc.\
**Live site:** [villarica.net](https://villarica.net)

## Overview

Villarica is one of the Philippines' most established pawnshop chains, trusted since 1954 and operating over 600 branches nationwide. Its old website, however, was barebones — a simple item grid pairing a title with a short description for each service, with little else to sell the brand or explain what customers could actually expect. The redesign brief was to modernize the site into something that matched the trust Villarica had already built over seven decades, **without touching the brand's existing color palette or typography**, both of which are already established across the company's marketing materials.

## The Challenge

The redesign wasn't happening in a vacuum — it was a direct response to competitor Palawan Express, whose site had pulled ahead with stronger visuals, clearer service presentation, and full mobile responsiveness. The client's mandate was to not just catch up but **surpass** Palawan Express visually, while keeping two constraints fixed: retain Villarica's existing blue-and-red brand identity and font, and expand the content so every service (Sangla, Cash Padala, Cash Withdrawal, Money Changer, Pay Bills, Shop Jewelry, Loading Station, and more) was explained thoroughly rather than reduced to a one-line blurb. The task was to add visual sophistication and depth of content on top of a brand system that couldn't change.

## Design Approach

- **Glassmorphism, at the client's request.** Villarica specifically asked for a glassmorphic direction — frosted-glass panels, soft shadows, and gradient washes sit behind hero sections and content cards across the site. Beyond modernizing the look, it makes the site softer to look at, and the added depth gives the pages a sense of life rather than sitting flat, all without altering the existing brand colors themselves.
- **Out-of-bounds diamond-cutout imagery, rooted in the business itself.** Photos are cropped into diamond frames that break out of their containers at an angle, replacing the rectangular photo blocks of the old site with a shape that feels distinct and intentional. The motif is a nod to Villarica's core pawnshop business of buying and selling jewelry — the diamond shape ties directly back to that, with its corners rounded off so it reads as friendly and approachable rather than sharp.
- **Full service pages instead of grid tiles.** Each service that used to live as a title-and-blurb tile in a grid now has its own page — Cash Padala, for example, walks through how to send money and how to receive money as separate numbered processes, lists domestic and international remittance partners, and shows accepted valid IDs.
- **Brand storytelling added where there was none.** The old site had no real About Us page; the redesign adds the company's founding story from 1954, its mission and vision, three core values (Mapagkakatiwalaan, Maasahan, Malalapitan), and a milestone timeline — content that didn't exist before but builds the trust the brand has earned.
- **Mobile responsiveness built in from the start.** Directly answering the gap that let Palawan Express pull ahead, every template — hero sections, diamond image crops, service breakdowns, branch finder — was built to reflow cleanly on mobile.

## Outcome

The result keeps Villarica's brand instantly recognizable — same colors, same fonts, same voice — while looking and functioning like a site built years ahead of the one it replaced. What used to be a flat grid of titles and one-line descriptions is now a set of fully fleshed-out service pages, wrapped in a glassmorphic visual language of gradients, blurs, and diamond-cut photography that gives Villarica a stronger visual footing against Palawan Express, on desktop and mobile alike.
